# Third-Party Notices

## Open-Meteo

Weather data is provided by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) and is made available
under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

This application requests a seven-day daily forecast and presents provider weather codes,
temperatures, precipitation totals, and precipitation probabilities in a gardening-focused
interface. Labels, icons, caching, fallback-location messaging, and garden-job associations
are modifications and presentation choices made by this application.

The maintained application uses Open-Meteo's free endpoint for non-commercial operation.
Commercial deployments must obtain a suitable provider license or replace the integration.
